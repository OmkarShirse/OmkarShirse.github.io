SYSTEM_01 · RESEARCH PUBLICATION IJRESM JAN 2026

Smart Egg Incubation & Biological Microclimates

Avian embryonic development demands strict microclimate regulation. A fluctuation of ±1.5°C or dry air halts cellular development. This autonomous closed-loop system holds 37.5°C ±0.2°C and regulates dual-stage humidity (58% vs. 70% RH) over a 21-day timeline using non-blocking ESP8266 RTOS scheduling and Blynk IoT cloud synchronization.

SYSTEM_03 · BARE-METAL FIRMWARE 18.5 MHZ REGISTER

ESP32 Fast GPIO Driver (Zero Spinlock)

Standard ESP-IDF gpio_set_level() enters a spinlock critical section (portENTER_CRITICAL) on every call, limiting pin toggling to ~1.8 MHz. This driver writes directly to the ESP32 hardware W1TS (0x3FF44004) and W1TC (0x3FF44008) atomic registers with IRAM_ATTR inlining, driving throughput to 18.5 MHz (10.2x speedup) with zero race hazards.

SYSTEM_04 · SILICON RTL DESIGN VHDL · ARTIX-7

FPGA Full-Duplex UART Controller

Synthesizable 8-N-1 serial communications core designed from gate primitives in VHDL. Implements fractional baud rate division, 16x oversampling receiver state machines with center-bit majority voting across ticks 7, 8, and 9 to eliminate noise, and a 2-stage flip-flop synchronizer preventing clock domain metastability.

Engineering Manifesto · Omkar Shirse

Technology should be quiet, reliable, and grounded in the physical reality of silicon.

Modern computing layers abstraction upon abstraction, introducing uncontrolled latency, power dissipation, and unpredictable memory overhead. I study Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at MGM's College of Engineering, Nanded (graduating Nov 2026) to build systems with deterministic clarity.

Whether developing biological microclimate control published in IJRESM, executing neural transformers on 520 KB RAM without dynamic memory fragmentation, or bypassing HAL spinlocks for 18.5 MHz atomic GPIO, my work proves that true performance comes from understanding the physics of silicon.
